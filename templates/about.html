About Prodlysis

AI-Powered UX Analysis & Product Insights for data teams

What is Prodlysis?

Prodlysis is an AI-powered analytics comparison platform that helps anyone building and analyzing product data compare reports across different periods, identify UX improvements, calculate percentage changes in key metrics, and generate actionable recommendations.

Instead of manually comparing dashboards, you upload or paste data from two time periods (from any analytics platform) and Prodlysis automatically:

  • Detects UX issues
  • Compares previous and current performance
  • Calculates improvements or regressions
  • Explains likely causes with confidence scores
  • Suggests UI improvements
  • Generates executive-ready reports (PDF / Markdown / clipboard)

Vision

Become the easiest AI-powered product analytics companion for anyone who builds, ships, and analyzes product data.

Who it's for

  • Product Managers · Associate PMs · Product Owners
  • UX Designers · Product Analysts · Data Analysts
  • Startup Founders · Growth Teams · Engineers

How it works

  1. Upload reports from any analytics platform (CSV / JSON) or paste metrics.
  2. Compare automatically — percentage changes, improved/regressed status.
  3. Analyze — AI findings with evidence, likely causes, and confidence.
  4. Recommend — prioritized, actionable UI improvements.
  5. Export — presentation-ready PDF or Markdown report.

Supported metrics

Sessions · Drop-offs · Drop-off Rate · Rage Clicks · Dead Clicks · Scroll Depth · Session Duration · Bounce Rate · Conversion Rate

Works with any analytics source

Upload CSVs or paste metrics from Microsoft Clarity, Google Analytics, Mixpanel, PostHog, Hotjar, Amplitude, and more — or your own spreadsheets.

Roadmap

  • Phase 2: Direct platform integrations, heatmaps, funnels, session recording summaries, weekly email reports.
  • Phase 3: GA4, Mixpanel, Hotjar, PostHog, Amplitude, Jira/Linear ticket creation, Slack notifications.
